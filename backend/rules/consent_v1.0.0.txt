TITLE=Telegram Threat Intelligence (TTI) Informed Consent Charter

1. CORE MISSION & GUARANTEES:
The TTI Platform assists cybersecurity researchers and consented individuals in detecting
fraud, scams, phishing, and online extortion.
We strictly operate under the following invariants:
- NO HIDDEN SURVEILLANCE: We do not scrape private chats, unauthorized channels, or non-consenting users.
- TELEGRAM PRIVACY MODE: The bot cannot see or read messages from non-consenting users in groups.
- ZERO RETENTION WITHOUT CONSENT: Messages from non-consenting users in approved groups are dropped immediately.
- PURPOSE-SPECIFIC & REVOCABLE: You choose exactly what is analyzed. You can revoke consent at any time.

2. GRANULAR PERMISSIONS (ALL DEFAULT OFF):
- message_analysis: Authorizes analyzing text you submit via /submit for fraud and threat indicators.
- keyword_detection: Authorizes scanning your consented text against threat keyword dictionaries.
- nlp_analysis: Authorizes extracting entities (UPI IDs, bank IFSC, URLs, phone numbers) from consented text.
- activity_analytics: Authorizes calculating your own message frequency baselines (never comparing against others).
- timeline: Authorizes chronological threat activity timeline generation.
- reports: Authorizes including your consented threat telemetry in formal case reports.
- approved_group_context: Authorizes processing messages you post in verified, monitored groups.

3. YOUR STATUTORY RIGHTS:
- Review your active consent and toggle permissions at any time with /consent.
- Revoke all processing immediately with /revoke.
- Export all data stored about you with /export.
- Permanently purge your records and telemetry with /delete_me.
